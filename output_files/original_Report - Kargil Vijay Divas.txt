Report : Celebration of 25th Kargil Vijay Diwas
● Date: 26th July 2024
● Time: The event was conducted from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
Venue: Rukhmini Hall, MGM University, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar
● Organized by: JNEC NCC MGM University, Territorial Army, Matru Bhoomi (Jaswant
Singh Group), NCC Cadets, Municipal Corporation of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Sainik
Welfare Association, MGM Schools.
Introduction
On the 26th of July 2024, the 25th Kargil Vijay Diwas was celebrated with great
enthusiasm and reverence at Rukmini Auditorium, MGM University, Chhatrapati
Sambhajinagar. This event marked a significant milestone in commemorating the valor,
dedication, and supreme sacrifices of the Indian Armed Forces during the Kargil War of
1999.
The celebration was a collaborative effort of MGM University, the Territorial Army, Matru
Bhoomi (Jaswant Singh Group), NCC Cadets, the Municipal Corporation of Chhatrapati
Sambhajinagar, the Sainik Welfare Association, and MGM Schools. The primary aim was
to honor the Kargil heroes, educate the younger generation about their sacrifices, and
foster a spirit of patriotism among participants.

Event Description
● Opening Ceremony
The event began with a warm welcome to the dignitaries, followed by the lighting of the
ceremonial lamp. A floral tribute was paid to the martyrs, accompanied by a moment of
silence. The national anthem marked the official start of the event.
● Cultural Performances
Patriotic dance performances depicted the bravery of Indian soldiers, evoking deep
emotions among the audience.Musical renditions of iconic patriotic songs like “Teri Mitti”
and “Ae Watan” captured the spirit of the day.
Motivational Speeches
● Keynote Address by Brig. U. K. Ojha: The Group Commander’s speech captivated
the audience as he elaborated on the importance of discipline, unity, and patriotism in
building a strong and resilient nation.
● Veteran Narratives: Kargil soldier Ashok Hange shared his personal stories of the
Kargil War, describing the challenges faced during the operation and the camaraderie
that kept the troops motivated.
● Inspirational Speech by Matru Bhoomi founder Jaswant Singh: He spoke about his
organization's role in supporting soldiers and their families and urged the audience to
contribute to the nation's development
Educational Activities
A documentary on the Kargil War was screened, offering insights into the strategies and
operations that led to India’s victory.
Felicitations
The dignitaries, including Brig. U. K. Ojha and Kargil War veterans were honored with
mementos as a token of gratitude for their service. The organizing committee and cadets
were also commended for their dedication and effort in ensuring the event’s success.

Observations and Impact
The event was highly impactful, leaving a profound impression on all attendees. The cultural
performances, coupled with the motivational speeches and educational segments,
effectively conveyed the sacrifices made by the armed forces.
The participation of Kargil veterans added authenticity and depth to the program. Their
stories of resilience and valor inspired the audience to uphold the values of courage,
determination, and patriotism.
The collaborative efforts of MGM University, the Territorial Army, Matru Bhoomi, NCC
Cadets, the Municipal Corporation of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, the Sainik Welfare
Association, and MGM Schools ensured the seamless execution of the event.

Conclusion
The 25th Kargil Vijay Diwas celebration at MGM University was a fitting tribute to the
heroes of the Kargil War. It not only honored the martyrs and veterans but also educated
and inspired the younger generation to contribute meaningfully to the nation's progress.
The program’s success underscored the importance of such commemorative events in
fostering national unity and respect for the armed forces.
