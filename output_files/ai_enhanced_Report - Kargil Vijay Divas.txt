Now, for the introduction and the spirit of the day.

On the twenty-sixth of July, twenty twenty-four, the twenty-fifth Kargil Vijay Diwas was celebrated with great enthusiasm and deep reverence at Rukmini Auditorium, within MGM University, in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. This event marked a very significant milestone. It was held to commemorate the valor, the dedication, and the supreme sacrifices made by the Indian Armed Forces during the Kargil War back in nineteen ninety-nine.

The entire celebration was truly a joint effort. It involved MGM University, the Territorial Army, Matru Bhoomi, the Jaswant Singh Group, NCC Cadets, the Municipal Corporation of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, the Sainik Welfare Association, and MGM Schools. The main goal was clear: to honor the Kargil heroes, to educate the younger generation about their incredible sacrifices, and to foster a strong spirit of patriotism among all the participants.

Next, there was an inspirational speech given by Matru Bhoomi founder, Jaswant Singh. He spoke about his organization’s dedicated role in supporting soldiers and their families. He also passionately urged the audience to contribute actively to the nation's ongoing development.

The educational activities provided historical context. A documentary on the Kargil War was screened. This offered valuable insights into the strategies and operations that ultimately led to India’s victory in that conflict.

The day also included moments of recognition. The felicitation ceremony honored many important people. The dignitaries, including Brigadier U. K.
